Featured project

I examined whether NFL teams with better field-goal percentages won more games during the 2025 regular season. The analysis combines official nflverse team statistics and schedules for all 32 teams.

Question

Did NFL teams that made a higher percentage of their field goals also win more games during the 2025 regular season?

Method

Python, pandas, correlation, linear regression, and two explanatory visualizations.

Finding

r = 0.14

A weak, statistically non-significant relationship.
