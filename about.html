About me and reflection

Athlete, builder, and developing analyst.

I am a Sports Analytics student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. My interest in sports began through years of competing in baseball and basketball. I pitched for Greensboro College and now serve as vice president of the Charlotte 49ers Club Baseball Team. Those experiences taught me that good decisions depend on preparation, communication, and the ability to respond when a plan changes.

Outside the classroom, I have developed leadership and problem-solving skills through hands-on work. As a lead renovator with Hawkins Carpentry, I helped plan and complete residential projects while communicating directly with clients. As a pool technician and junior partner with Premier Pool Management, I handled maintenance, repairs, customer education, inventory, marketing, and sales support. Both roles required careful attention to detail and accountability for the quality of the final result.

I am now learning how to apply those same habits to data. Python, pandas, and visualization tools give me a way to move from a sports question to a defensible answer. In my first end-to-end analysis, I investigated whether NFL teams with higher field-goal percentages won more games. The project helped me practice collecting data, documenting cleaning decisions, testing a relationship, and explaining why a weak result is still useful.

My career interests sit at the intersection of sports analytics, media, and communication. I want to become someone who can understand the numbers and turn them into stories that coaches, athletes, fans, or organizations can use. This portfolio will continue to grow as I strengthen my technical skills, complete more projects, and learn to communicate evidence with clarity and honesty.
