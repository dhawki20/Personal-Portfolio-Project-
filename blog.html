What I learned from my first NFL data analysis

When I started my field-goal project, I expected accurate kicking to have a clearer connection with winning. The data showed only a weak positive relationship. That reminded me that good analysis is about explaining what the evidence actually shows, not forcing the result I expected.

The project also showed me how much work happens before making a chart. I had to clean team labels, calculate records from the schedule, combine two datasets, and check that all 32 teams were included.

My next step is to study a more specific question, such as whether field-goal performance matters more in one-score games. That would add game context that a full-season team average cannot capture.
